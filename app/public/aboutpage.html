ABOUT

Behind Blokkers is Danish Pinkorange Studio, which dedicates their work to creating thoughtful and influential design solutions. We have a love for colors and know of their positive impact on people. Therefore, we want, with our design, to show that one should not be afraid to put colors together in different ways. The posters combine modern and contrasting color combinations with a geometric design, which results in a bold and timeless design. Here at our webshop, you put the finishing touches on the design through your own choice of letters, shapes and colors. We hope you will love creating the final look just as much as we have loved perfecting the graphic elements and amazing color combinations. And then we hope the final poster will bring joy to the nursery, office, living room or anywhere else in your home.
