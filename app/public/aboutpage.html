NOW YOU BUILD THE POSTER

BLOKKERS is a modern poster concept designed by the danish design duo Pinkorange Studio who makes sustainable and influential solutions come to life. The posters combine unique and contrasting colors with a geometric design resulting in a bold yet timeless look. At BLOKKERS we hope that your one-of-a-kind poster will bring joy to the nursery, office, living room or anywhere else in your home.

“We have a love for colors and their impact on people. The goal with our design is to show that you shouldn’t be afraid of frisky and brave color combinations”
