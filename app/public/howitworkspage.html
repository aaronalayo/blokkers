How it works

Here at our webshop you put the finishing touches on the posters through your own choice of letters, shapes, colors and sizes. With 29 letters in 8 different color schemes there are more than a billion different combinations. The sky is quite literally the limit! We hope you’ll love creating your own poster as much as we’ve loved perfecting the building blocks.

1. Pick your prefered size between our 3 options A3 (297 x 420 mm), A2 (420 x 594 mm) and A1 (594 x 841 mm). Your poster will be printed by professionals on semi matte 170 g. FSC®-certified photo paper with vibrant high quality colors and vegetable inks. We’re committed to environmental protection with a modern and sustainable use of paper. Posters are printed and shipped from Denmark also ensuring low environmental impact.

2. Now use the on-screen keyboard to type whatever you want. It can be your name, hometown, a quote you love or simply a random word. The graphic form of the letters and the 8 different color variations allow you to create endless shapes and patterns. Explore the opportunities and have fun with it! Need inspiration? Check out our instagram.

3. After creating your final design we’ll ask you if you’re satisfied - If yes you can complete the order by filling out your contact, shipping and payment information. We accept all cards and Mobile- Pay for a secure and easy transaction.

4. Shortly after completing the order you’ll receive a confirmation by email confirming product and shipping details. If you have no objections to the information stated in the email all there’s left to do is to wait for your package to arrive.

5. When you receive your poster hang it somewhere nice from where it can spark happiness to everyone who passes it. It’s our recommendation that you place it in a frame to protect it from poten- tial damage caused by sunlight, dirt or humidity. We would love to see your final design hanging in your home! Share it on your socials or DM us a photo at @blokkersdk.
